Intellectual honesty, because the tax isn't always guilty. Waiting is the right call when:
  • You don't have the down payment yet — buying with 3% down and PMI you can't afford is worse than waiting a year.
  • Waiting one more year gets you to 20% down and kills PMI — run both scenarios and compare.
  • Your job, city, or life is genuinely uncertain — a house is an anchor; don't drop it in a storm.
  • You're repairing credit — a 1% better rate from a cleaner score can outweigh a year of appreciation.
Waiting is the wrong call when the reason is a vibe — "the market feels high," "maybe rates drop," "I'll know when I know." The meter doesn't care about vibes.
